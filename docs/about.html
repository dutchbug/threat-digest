About

Daily security news, threat research and actively exploited vulnerabilities, collected automatically. Weekly analysis written by me.

Every morning a scheduled GitHub Actions job collects the sources below, removes duplicates, tags items by topic, sector and regulation, highlights technology on my watchlist and rebuilds this site. Weekly analysis posts are written by me.

Filters and profiles

Use the filter panel to narrow the list by topic, sector, regulation or source type. Profiles are saved filter sets, for example for a specific job or industry. Preset profiles:

  • Healthcare / HIPAA
  • Payments / PCI DSS
  • GRC and compliance
  • SOC analyst
  • Microsoft 365 admin
  • Vulnerability management
  • Public sector and education
  • Threat research only

You can save your own profiles; they are stored in your browser only. Sector and regulation tags come from keyword matching and are hints, not a compliance assessment.
